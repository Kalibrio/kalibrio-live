About

A venture lab for playful ideas

Kalibrio Limited is an independent creative company founded by Ludovic Bodin in Hong Kong. We develop original intellectual properties and turn promising ideas into games, books, music, and digital experiences.

We work with a small network of artists, developers, writers, and creative partners, assembling the right team for each project.
