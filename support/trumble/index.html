Frequently asked

How do I restore my progress on a new phone?

On your old phone, open Settings → Cloud save and note your 6-character save code. On the new phone, open the same screen and enter the code — your progress comes back.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple account, so make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought them with.

How do lives refill?

Lives refill automatically over time — one life at a time, up to the maximum. You can also earn or buy refills in the game.

How do I remove ads?

The Remove Ads purchase in the shop turns off ad breaks permanently. If you've already bought it and ads reappear (for example on a new phone), use Restore Purchases.

How do I report a leaderboard name?

If you see an offensive nickname on the leaderboard, email us with the name and country shown and we'll review it promptly.
