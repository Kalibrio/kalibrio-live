Frequently asked

How do I play?

Dig tunnels through the sand so birds fall and meet. Two identical birds merge into the next bird. Reach each level's goal before you run out of moves.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Open the pause menu and choose Transfer Progress. Copy the code, then paste it into the same screen on the new device. Keep the code private.

What are races and flocks?

Daily races rank players who chase the same goals. Flocks are small groups you create or join with an invite code. Both show your player name and score.

How do I report a player name?

Email us the name and where you saw it, and we'll review it promptly.
