How to play

Gems fall into the well. Drag to slide them left or right. Line up three or more gems of the same kind to merge them into a bigger gem, and chain merges for bonus points. Reach each stage's goal before time runs out. You can also play in your browser at prismdrop.kalibrio.com.

Frequently asked

Does Prism Drop have ads or purchases?

No. The game is free, with no in-game ads and no in-app purchases.

Why does the game ask to track me?

We advertise Prism Drop online. If you allow tracking, the game tells Google Ads that a new player started playing, so we can see which ads work. Saying no changes nothing in the game. Details are in the privacy policy.
