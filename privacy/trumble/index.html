Trumble — Privacy Policy

Kalibrio Limited · Effective 1 September 2026 · Applies to Trumble on iPhone and on the web (trumble.kalibrio.com)

Trumble is a puzzle game made by Kalibrio Limited, an independent creative company registered in Hong Kong. Trumble is designed so you can simply play: there are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no collection of personal information.

What we collect

  • A random device identifier. When you first play, Trumble generates a short random string and stores it on your device. It is not derived from your name, your email, any hardware identifier, or any Apple identifier, and it cannot be used to identify you as a person.
  • Gameplay telemetry. Events such as levels played, wins and losses, session length, and which features you use, tied to that random identifier. We use this solely to understand which levels are too hard, where the game is confusing, and how to make Trumble better.
  • Leaderboard entries. If you join the leaderboard, we store the nickname you choose, your score, and the country code you pick. Choose any nickname you like — it does not need to be your real name.
  • Team / crew data (optional). If you join or create a team, we store the team's name and membership, tied to the same random identifier.
  • Cloud save (optional). If you turn on cloud save, we store a backup of your game progress (game state only) so you can restore it on another device with your save code.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details
  • No location; no contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs — analytics are first-party only
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

Where data lives, and for how long

  • On your device: your game progress, settings, and the random identifier are stored locally.
  • On our servers: the data above is sent to servers operated by Kalibrio Limited, hosted in the United States. It is not shared with, or processed by, any third-party analytics service.
  • Retention: gameplay telemetry is aggregated and rotated. Leaderboard entries and cloud saves persist while you use the game.

Advertising and purchases

  • Trumble may show house ads — screens promoting our own products only. No ad network is integrated, and no data is shared with advertisers. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships.
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details.

Children

Trumble is rated 4+. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • Local data: delete the app (or clear your browser's site data for trumble.kalibrio.com). This removes your progress and the random identifier.
  • Server data: email support@kalibrio.com with your save code or leaderboard nickname, and we will delete the telemetry, leaderboard entries, and cloud save associated with your device. See data requests for the simple steps.

Changes

If Trumble's data practices change — for example, if a future update adds a third-party service — we will name the service here and update the effective date before the change ships.
