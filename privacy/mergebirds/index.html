Merge Birds — Privacy Policy

Kalibrio Limited · Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to Merge Birds on iPhone and on the web (mergebirds.kalibrio.com)

Merge Birds is a puzzle game made by Kalibrio Limited, an independent creative company registered in Hong Kong. You do not need an account, real name or email address to play. The game uses a random installation identifier to link gameplay records from the same installation. This is pseudonymous information, not anonymous data.

What we collect

  • A random device identifier. When you first play, the game generates a random string and stores it on your device. It is not derived from your name, email, any hardware identifier or any Apple identifier.
  • Gameplay telemetry. Events such as levels played, wins and losses, session length and features used, tied to that identifier. We use it only to tune difficulty and improve the game.
  • Race and flock leaderboards. Your player name and scores are sent to our leaderboard service and can be shown to other players in daily races and in flocks (groups you create or join with an invite code). Do not put personal information in your player name.

What we do not collect

  • No real name, email address or phone number is needed to play. We only receive what you choose to put in a player name or a support email.
  • The game does not request location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. Our servers receive your IP address as part of any network request.
  • The iPhone app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs, and does not use the Apple advertising identifier. We do not sell your data.

Where data lives, and for how long

  • On your device: your progress, settings and the random identifier.
  • On our servers: telemetry and leaderboard entries, on servers operated by Kalibrio Limited. They are not shared with any third party.
  • Retention: server records have no automatic expiry today. Deleting the app does not delete them; ask us and we will.

Progress transfer

The transfer code shown in the game encodes your save on your device. Anyone with the code can copy your progress, so keep it private.

Purchases

The iPhone app has no real-money purchases. If that changes, purchases will go through Apple and this policy will be updated first.

The web version

On mergebirds.kalibrio.com the game may load advertising-measurement pixels (Meta, TikTok) and, if enabled, Google ad placements and Stripe checkout links. None of these are present in the iPhone app.

Children

The age rating describes the game's content; the data practices above still apply. Parents or guardians can contact support@kalibrio.com with questions or deletion requests.

Deleting your data

  • Local data: delete the app (or clear site data for mergebirds.kalibrio.com).
  • Server data: email support@kalibrio.com. We will delete the telemetry and leaderboard entries linked to your installation. See data requests.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, for example a new third-party service, we will name it here and update the effective date before the change ships.
