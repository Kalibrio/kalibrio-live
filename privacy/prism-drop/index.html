Prism Drop — Privacy Policy

Kalibrio Limited · Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to Prism Drop on iPhone and on the web (prismdrop.kalibrio.com)

Prism Drop is a puzzle game made by Kalibrio Limited, an independent company registered in Hong Kong. You do not need an account, a name or an email address to play. This policy explains the limited data the game sends and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Gameplay events. Events such as game start, stage completed, win, loss, replay and how long a session lasted, plus whether you came back the same day or the next day. We use them to understand whether the game is fun and how hard it is.
  • A random analytics identifier. Created on your device the first time you play and stored there. It is not derived from your name, email, Apple ID or any hardware identifier. It links events from the same installation or browser.
  • Technical information sent with every network request, such as your IP address, device type, operating-system version and language. Google may derive an approximate (city-level) location from the IP address.

Who processes it

  • Google Analytics 4 receives the gameplay events and the random identifier on our behalf. On iPhone, Google signals and ad personalization are switched off for these analytics.
  • Google Ads conversion measurement. We advertise Prism Drop with Google Ads. To learn which ads bring new players, the game can report a "first play" conversion to Google Ads. On iPhone this happens only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", no Google Ads tag is loaded and no conversion is sent. On the web, the conversion tag runs on prismdrop.kalibrio.com.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we do not collect

  • No account, real name, email address or phone number.
  • No GPS location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No in-app purchases and no in-game ads.
  • We do not sell your data.

Storage and retention

  • On your device: the random identifier and a small record of when you first and last played, used to count same-day and next-day returns. Deleting the app (or clearing site data on the web) removes them.
  • At Google: Google Analytics deletes event-level data automatically after the retention period set in our account, which is never longer than 14 months.

Your choices

  • On iPhone, you can change the tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On the web, you can block or clear cookies and site data in your browser, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • To ask about or delete data, see data requests or email support@kalibrio.com.

Children

Prism Drop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@kalibrio.com. Kalibrio Limited, Hong Kong.
